# KnowFlyZone design rules

Applies to the web app, the landing page, slides and Devpost.

## Never
1. Harsh or decorative gradients
2. Lucide (or any stock) icon sets
3. Pure white background
4. Rainbow coloring
5. Drop shadows on UI panels
6. Three feature cards in a row
7. Emojis
8. Liquid glass / frosted glass
9. Em dashes, in UI or copy
10. Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk
11. Colored left stripe on boxes
12. Fake testimonials
13. Bento grids
14. Terminal-window mockups
15. "It's not X, it's Y" copy
16. Checkmark bullets
17. Pricing tiers
18. Missing real product demo (always show the real thing)
19. Soft, large corner radius
20. Purple and black
21. Missing skeleton loaders (loading states must use them)
22. Radial orbs / glow blobs
23. Dot grids
24. Sparkle icons
25. Animated arrows
26. Missing terms of service
27. Missing privacy policy
28. Gratuitous hover animations
29. Neon colors
30. Basic pastel colors

## Use instead
- **Color:** background `#0e1013`, text off-white `#e8ebef`, secondary `#8a929d`, hairlines `#23272d`.
  Accents are only the verdict colors: Go `#2f9e5b`, Wait `#d99a1e`, Don't fly `#d64545`.
- **Type:** B612 for text, B612 Mono for numbers and labels (Airbus cockpit typeface).
- **Shape:** corners 2-4 px, flat panels, 1 px dividers, no shadows.
- **Labels:** words, not icons ("Temperature", "Hawk alert", "Gemini").
- **Copy:** short, specific, plain. Say what happens. No em dashes.
- **Motion:** only when it carries meaning (assembly, propellers, demo walk). Hover = color change.
- **Loading:** skeleton placeholders shaped like the content they replace.
- **Legal:** real Privacy and Terms pages, linked in the footer.
