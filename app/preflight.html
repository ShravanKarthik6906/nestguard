      What works offline

      • Places you already checked. KnowFlyZone keeps the last result for each place and date. Offline, it shows that saved copy and marks it with the time it was saved, so you know it may be out of date.
      • Sample data. Built-in sample scenarios and the guided demo work with no connection at all. They are examples, not live conditions.
      • Spoken alerts. Recorded voice clips play offline; new sentences need a connection.
      • Not offline. New places, live weather, the map background and the controller device need a connection.

      When the connection returns

      KnowFlyZone reloads the latest data for your spot automatically and updates the assessment.
